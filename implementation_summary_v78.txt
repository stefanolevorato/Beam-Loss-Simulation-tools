Charged transport increment

  queries along the straight track. Vacuum gaps are crossed without energy

  path length, kinetic energy before/after, and deposited energy.

  apportions loss uniformly per areal thickness across the traversed solids.
  Residual energy after leaving all configured solids is recorded as charged
  escape. Segment deposits are assigned to their actual volumes.

  charged, escaped gamma, sub-threshold terminated gamma, and balance residual.
  Gamma interaction counts are separate from charged material-loss segments.

  retaining compatibility with earlier aggregate charged-track records.
